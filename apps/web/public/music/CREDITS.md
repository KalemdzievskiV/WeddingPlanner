# Invitation music

Background tracks couples can pick for the designed invitation (decisions 130–131). All are free to use
without credit. Converted to 96 kbps MP3, loudness-normalised, with a 1.5 s fade-in.

| File | Piece | Performer | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `jesu.mp3` | J. S. Bach – Jesu, Joy of Man's Desiring (BWV 147/10) | Orchestra Gli Armonici | CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bach,_BWV_147,_10._Jesus_bleibet_meine_Freude.ogg |
| `gymnopedie.mp3` | Erik Satie – Gymnopédie No. 1 | Robin Alciatore | Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erik_Satie_-_gymnopedies_-_la_1_ere._lent_et_douloureux.ogg |
| `ave-maria.mp3` | Franz Schubert – Ave Maria (D. 839) | Free Tim | CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Free_Tim_-_Schuberts_Ave_Maria.ogg |

To add a track: put `<id>.mp3` here, add `{ id, title }` to `INVITE_TRACKS` in `packages/shared/src/planner-schemas.ts`,
and record its source and license above.
